## Description

Cardi tells you what to wear for the hours you’re actually outside, not just right now.

The forecast says 14°. But at 8am, in the wind, on your walk to the station, it feels like 9°. Cardi looks at your trip out and your trip home and turns them into one simple answer: what to wear, and what to bring.

ONE GLANCE BEFORE YOU LEAVE
• Your feels like, tuned to you
• Today’s outfit, in words that fit how you dress
• Brolly, layers and SPF, only when you need them

ON YOUR LOCK SCREEN
Add Cardi to your Lock Screen or Home Screen and you won’t need to open the app. The widget changes through the day: your morning outfit, the trip home, then tomorrow.

A MORNING HEADS-UP
Pick a time and get one line each morning, like “Feels 9° · Jacket day. Brolly for the way home.”

A FEELS LIKE THAT’S YOURS
Tell Cardi if you run hot or cold, when you leave and get home, and how you get about. Tap “bit chilly” or “too warm” and it adjusts. The more you use it, the more it fits you.

PLAN THE WEEK
See every day at a glance, and tap any day for the full plan, there and back.

ALSO COVERS
• Hair days: a frizz forecast for curly, wavy or straight hair (switch it off if you like)
• UV and SPF reminders on cool, bright days
• Cycling, walking or the bus
• Drying days for your washing

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your settings stay on your phone.

## App Review notes

No account or login is needed. Location is optional: on the location step, tap "Pick a city instead" and search for any city. The widget and morning notification can be tested after setup. Weather data comes from Apple WeatherKit.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Cardi sends anonymous usage events (Aptabase), so the answer is no longer "Data Not Collected". Answer:

1. **Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** Yes.
2. Tick **Usage Data → Product Interaction** only.
3. For Product Interaction: **Analytics**: yes. Everything else: no.
4. **Linked to the user's identity?** No.
5. **Used for tracking?** No.

The label then reads "Data Not Linked to You: Usage Data". Location is not collected: coordinates go to Apple Weather (or Open-Meteo) only to fetch the forecast and Cardi doesn't keep them.
